# Contributing

Short version: **fork it freely, but pull requests here cannot be merged.**

This repository is generated. The deck is authored in a private repository and written out
to this one by a script, so anything committed here is overwritten by the next export. That
is a property of the setup rather than a policy about your change.

**If you want to use the talk** — give it, cut it down, translate it, restyle it — fork and
go. The content is CC BY 4.0; the README's "Adapt it" section points at the few files worth
knowing about. You do not need permission and you do not need to tell anyone.

**If you found a mistake** — a wrong formula, a broken build, a claim that does not hold, a
figure that says something different from its caption — please **open an issue**. Those are
read, and a fix upstream reaches everyone at the next export. A patch in an issue comment is
very welcome; it just has to be applied on the other side.

Worth reporting even though it looks like a typo: anything where a **number disagrees with a
picture**. The deck's figures are drawn to the maths, so a mismatch is a real defect rather
than a rendering quirk.

Not worth reporting: the results tables' values. They are deliberately fake, watermarked as
such, and are not meant to be reproducible.
